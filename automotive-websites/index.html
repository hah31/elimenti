Automotive website design for clubs, events and shops

Elimenti designs websites for the parts of the car world that sell on trust and taste: car clubs, car shows and concours, collector car storage, and restoration and custom shops. Sites are built on WordPress so you can update them, or custom-coded, for clients anywhere in the US. What car people look for on a site

Enthusiasts are a hard audience. They notice a stock photo of the wrong model year, and they leave when a gallery takes ten seconds to load on a phone in a paddock. A few things hold across every site on this page:

  • The cars come first. Large photographs, correctly captioned, sized so they load quickly on a phone signal.
  • Details are right. Year, make, model and chassis details are written the way an owner would write them.
  • Privacy is designed in. Plates, VINs, home garages and owners' names stay off the site unless the owner says otherwise.
  • Dates and places are easy to find. A meet, a show or an open house gets its own page with the date, the address and what to expect.
  • History stays up. Past events, past builds and cars that have moved on are part of the record, so they get archived, not deleted.

Questions we get

Do you build car dealership websites?

Not inventory-driven dealer sites. A dealership needs live inventory, pricing and finance forms tied to its dealer system, and platforms made for that do it well. We build for clubs, events, storage facilities and restoration and custom shops, where the site is about the cars, the work and the people.

Can you build a parts or merchandise store?

We don't build online stores. If you already sell through a store such as Shopify, the site links to it clearly, so visitors can buy without the site having to take payments.

We're not near Chicago. Does that matter?

No. Elimenti is based in Hinsdale, Illinois, and every project runs on video calls, so we work with clubs, organizers and shops anywhere in the US.

Our kind of business isn't on this page. Will you still take it on?

Probably. Detailing and paint protection studios, private collections and racing teams need many of the same things. Tell us what you do and we'll say on the first call whether we're the right fit.
