Site Care: WordPress maintenance and website care

Site Care is Elimenti's WordPress maintenance and website care, for businesses in Hinsdale, across DuPage County and Chicago's western suburbs, and anywhere in the US. Edits, new pages, events, prices and photos are turned around in five business days, on a monthly plan or billed by the hour. On WordPress sites, the monthly plan also keeps WordPress, the theme and the plugins updated and backed up. It covers the WordPress and custom-coded sites we build and existing WordPress sites built by someone else, including page-builder sites made with Elementor, so you can keep a working site current without rebuilding it.

Ask about Site Care

Monthly
The plan we recommend, for businesses whose site changes regularly. It covers up to four updates a month, each an hour or less. On WordPress sites, it also covers WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, plus backups, and those don't count toward the four.
Hourly
Billed by the hour, for one-off edits. You get an estimate before the work starts. On a WordPress site, updates and backups aren't included on hourly care; we do them at the hourly rate when you ask.

Your rate
In writing before care starts, whichever way you pay.
Not included

What Site Care covers

  • Edits. Hours, prices, staff, services, menus, policies: anything in the copy that has changed.
  • New pages and sections. A new treatment, a finished project, a new service line, an event.
  • Photos. New images sized and compressed so the site stays fast.
  • Search basics. Titles, descriptions and structured data for every new page, the same standard as launch.
  • Small layout changes. Moving, adding or retiring a section without redesigning the page.
  • WordPress maintenance. On the monthly plan, WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, plus backups, so the site stays current and there's a copy to restore if an update goes wrong. They don't count toward your four monthly updates. On hourly care, they're billed by the hour.

For sites we build

On WordPress, you can make everyday edits yourself with your own login: text, photos, events and posts. Site Care handles what you'd rather not: the updates and backups WordPress needs, new pages and sections, and anything that touches the layout.

On a custom-coded site, there's no dashboard to log into and nothing to update. When something needs to change, you email it to us, and it's live within five business days.

Either way, you talk to the person who built the site, not a ticket queue.

For existing WordPress sites

Plenty of businesses have a WordPress site that works and just needs someone to keep it current. We take those on, built by any firm, including sites made with a page builder like Elementor. Before changing anything, we learn how the theme, page builder and plugins fit together, and we make sure a current backup exists.

That's how we work with ABN Construction Group, a general contractor in Lombard, in DuPage County. Its existing WordPress site runs on two page builders, Elementor and Neuron. We give an hour estimate before every task, and a new Automotive section is in progress. For Burdi Clothing in Hinsdale, we make targeted edits to its existing site, such as event pages for its car shows.

Questions about WordPress maintenance and Site Care

What does WordPress maintenance include?

Keeping the software current and recoverable: WordPress itself, the theme and every plugin get their updates, and backups mean there's a copy to restore if an update goes wrong. On the Site Care monthly plan, that work is included and doesn't count toward the plan's four monthly updates. On hourly care, it's done at the hourly rate whenever you ask for it. Hosting, the domain and any premium plugin or theme licenses stay in your name and are paid to those providers directly.

Do I need the monthly plan?

No. The monthly plan suits businesses whose site changes most months, and it covers up to four updates a month, each an hour or less. On a WordPress site, it also covers updates and backups, which is the main reason to choose it there. If you only need an occasional change, pay by the hour for the work you ask for, with an estimate first, including any WordPress updates you want done.

Can you take over a WordPress site someone else built?

Yes. We maintain existing WordPress sites built by other firms, including sites made with a page builder like Elementor. We start by learning the theme, page builder and plugins and confirming there's a current backup, then we make changes without touching what already works. ABN Construction Group's site, which we care for, runs on two page builders, Elementor and Neuron. On the monthly plan, we also keep WordPress, the theme and the plugins updated and backed up. If maintenance would cost more than a rebuild, we'll tell you.

Which areas does Site Care cover?

Elimenti is based in Hinsdale, in DuPage County. We take on WordPress sites across DuPage County, from Oak Brook, Clarendon Hills and Elmhurst to Lombard, Glen Ellyn and Naperville, and across the rest of Chicago's western suburbs, including Western Springs and Burr Ridge. Requests come by email and every change is confirmed once it's live, so Site Care works the same way for a business anywhere in the US.

Does Site Care include hosting?

No. Your hosting and domain stay in your name and on your own accounts, and you pay those providers directly. That way you're never locked in to Elimenti, and the site keeps running whether or not you're on a care plan. If you leave the plan, a WordPress site still needs its updates, so you or another developer would take those on.
