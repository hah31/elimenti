Web design for Chicago's western suburbs

Elimenti designs websites for high-end independent businesses across Chicago's western suburbs, including Burr Ridge, Western Springs, Clarendon Hills, Elmhurst, Glen Ellyn and Naperville, built on WordPress so you can edit them, or custom-coded. Projects start at $4,000 and run over video calls. Every town sells a little differently, so each section below covers what a website has to do there.

Towns with their own page

  • Hinsdale. Our home base and where our client work started: targeted website edits for Burdi Clothing on Hinsdale Avenue.
  • Oak Brook. Corporate headquarters, professional firms and Oakbrook Center. Clients who check a firm out before they call.

Burr Ridge

Burr Ridge pairs a lifestyle center with office parks and large residential lots. Burr Ridge Village Center gathers restaurants, shops and wellness businesses, including the Kohler Waters Spa, in one place. For a spa, salon or restaurant there, the site competes with everything else in the center, so it has to give someone a reason to choose you before they arrive: what you do, what it costs, and how to book. Design-build and landscape firms serving the area's larger properties need the opposite, a site that shows finished work at its best. Websites for landscape firms →

Western Springs

Downtown Western Springs runs along Burlington and Hillgrove Avenues, with shops, cafes and restaurants, many of them locally owned, and the Theatre of Western Springs a few blocks away. Customers are often neighbors who already know your name. The site's job is practical: current hours, what's new, how to book, and proof that you look as good online as you do on the street. For the town's design studios, the portfolio is the pitch. Websites for interior designers →

Clarendon Hills

Clarendon Hills has a small downtown around its Metra station, and the village has spent the past few years planning what that downtown becomes next. The downtown is compact, so for anyone outside the village, a website is how they find a business there at all. For the builders and contractors who work on the village's homes, that means a gallery that does the selling. Websites for home builders →

Elmhurst

Downtown Elmhurst's City Centre is anchored by the York Theatre, which opened in 1924 and now runs ten screens, with independent galleries, boutiques and restaurants around it. It's a downtown people make an evening of, which means they plan it on a phone first. A boutique or restaurant there needs hours, location and what makes it worth the trip on the first screen. Websites for boutiques →

Glen Ellyn

Downtown Glen Ellyn's oldest commercial blocks, on Main Street between Crescent Boulevard and Pennsylvania Avenue, make up the Glen Ellyn Downtown North Historic District, added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2013. Twenty-eight of its thirty-four buildings count toward its historic character. For a shop, studio or restaurant in a district like that, the website should carry the same sense of place as the storefront: real photos of the space, current hours and a way to book or order from a phone. Websites for boutiques →

Naperville

Downtown Naperville has more than 300 businesses around the DuPage River and its 1.75-mile Riverwalk, according to the Downtown Naperville Alliance, with independent shops next to national chains. With that much choice, a business there gets compared side by side, often on a phone, often in the same evening. The independents that win look distinct and answer the obvious questions fast. Websites for med spas →

Chicago, and anywhere else

Every Elimenti project runs over video calls, so distance doesn't change the process or the price. We built the site for 1 of 1 Car Club, a Porsche club based in Chicago and Dallas, and we take projects anywhere in the US. Close to home, we know the towns, the customers and the competition.

How local sites hold up on a phone

In November 2026 we ran the websites of 150 independent businesses from the Hinsdale and Oak Brook chamber directories, some of them in neighboring suburbs, through Lighthouse, Google's open-source testing tool, on its phone settings. The western suburbs website report has the results, with no business named, and the fixes worth making first.
