Elimenti builds custom-coded websites and handles WordPress site care for independent businesses in Glen Ellyn, Illinois. Our work in Glen Ellyn is ongoing care of the website for ABN Construction Group, a general contractor on Roosevelt Road.

Our work in Glen Ellyn

ABN builds commercial, retail, hospitality and multifamily projects across Chicagoland and the Midwest. Its website runs on WordPress, built by another firm, and ABN chose to keep it. Our job is to keep it current and add to it without breaking what works.

That started with a full onboarding pass through the existing build before we changed a thing. Every task since comes with an hour estimate up front. The current job is an Automotive section that brings Real Automotive Group into ABN's site as the two companies combine. Read the ABN case study →

What contractor websites need

For a contractor, the project portfolio is the sales pitch. A developer or business owner wants to see the work, the scale and the names you've built for. New projects should go up soon after they're finished, and every update should go live without taking the rest of the site down. On page-builder WordPress sites, that means a current backup before any change, every time. Home builder websites →
