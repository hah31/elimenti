# Elimenti off-site kit v2

Paste-ready. Use alongside `off-site-kit.md` (v1: Business Profile setup, directories, review asks), which still applies. Everything here promotes the research report: `https://elimenti.com/research/western-suburbs-website-report-2026/`.

**Numbers to quote** (all from `seo/data/western-suburbs-2026-summary.json`, collected 2026-11-04): 150 independent businesses from the Hinsdale and Oak Brook chamber directories; median mobile performance score 46 out of 100; 53% scored under 50; 19% of sites showing a phone number didn't make it tappable; 38% had local business structured data; WordPress ran 49% of the sites that loaded. Never name a business, in any channel.

## 2. Five guest-post pitches built on the report

Check each outlet's contributor guidelines before sending; not every one takes outside pieces.

**1. "The 15-minute website check every Hinsdale business owner should run"**
For: The Hinsdalean (expert column) or Hinsdale Magazine.
Angle: the redesign-or-refresh scorecard, with the local numbers next to each check.
Pitch: "Local owners keep asking whether their website needs replacing. I tested 150 chamber members' sites on a phone and turned the checks into a 15-minute self-test any owner can run, with how their neighbors did on each one. About 700 words, no business named, no pitch for my services."

**2. "Your customers are trying to call you. Can they?"**
For: the Hinsdale Chamber and Greater Oak Brook Chamber member newsletters.
Angle: 19% of sites that showed a phone number didn't make it tappable, plus malformed tap-to-call links; the two-minute fix.
Pitch: "A short, practical piece for members: how to check, in two minutes, whether a phone customer can call you from your website, and how to fix it. Built on my study of 150 member websites. About 400 words."

**3. "What 150 local business websites taught me about speed on a phone"**
For: a regional business publication or local business blog.
Angle: median score 46, 53% under 50; what page weight and builders have to do with it; what owners can do without rebuilding.
Pitch: "I measured the mobile speed of 150 independent business websites in Hinsdale and Oak Brook with Google's Lighthouse. The results surprised me. I'd like to write up what slows them down and the fixes that don't require a new site."

**4. "WordPress, Wix or Squarespace? What local businesses actually run on"**
For: chamber newsletters, or a local small-business podcast or blog.
Angle: platform shares from the study (49% WordPress) and how each performed; how an owner should choose.
Pitch: "Owners ask me which platform to use. Instead of an opinion, I can show what 150 local businesses actually run on and how each platform's sites performed on a phone, and when each one is the right choice."

**5. "Local search for suburban boutiques: what the data says sites are missing"**
For: a retail or downtown-business audience (a downtown business association newsletter or a retail trade blog).
Angle: only 38% had local business structured data; tappable phones; event pages (the Burdi car-show pages as an example of what works); Google's own local ranking factors.
Pitch: "A practical guide for independent shops on showing up in local search, built on data from 150 local business websites and Google's published guidance. No jargon, a checklist at the end."
