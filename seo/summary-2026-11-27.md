## Decisions

1. **The schema validator refused requests today.** `validator.schema.org/validate` answered HTTP 405 to the POST that `validate_schema.py` sends (it worked on November 25). Instead every page's JSON-LD was checked offline against the current schema.org vocabulary (unknown types, unknown properties, properties used on the wrong type, values outside a property's range), the same class of check the validator runs; the check was tested against a deliberately broken sample first. It's now `seo/tools/schema_vocab_check.py`, listed with the other checks in `CLAUDE.md`.
2. **Step One Sites is named on the About page.** It's already in the schema's `alternateName` and steponesites.com redirects here, so saying it in words costs nothing and helps assistants connect the two names. Delete that sentence if you'd rather not mention it.
3. **No lastmod bump for head-only changes.** Every page's HTML changed (robots tag, schema), so the IndexNow workflow will submit every page, but the sitemap dates only move for the pages whose content changed.
4. **Nothing on the site argues with ChatGPT's stale "$4,000".** As `CLAUDE.md` says, recrawls fix it; the sitemap dates and IndexNow are the levers. The new FAQ and About facts give the recrawl the right answer to pick up.
5. **Not done, because they need your accounts:** the Business Profile, Clutch reviews, the Cloudflare rules (Glen Ellyn 301, the two-hop http://www redirect) and Search Console access. See `seo/todo-hashim.md`.
