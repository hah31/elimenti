# SEO pass, November 25, 2026: WordPress maintenance and AI search

Branch `seo-wp-care-ai-search-nov-2026`. Baseline measured before any change: `seo/baseline-2026-11-25.md`. Earlier passes: `seo/summary-2026-11-18.md`, `seo/summary-2026-11.md`.

## What the baseline showed

- **Google has the whole site.** All 46 sitemap URLs were in Google's index (21 of 37 on November 5).
- **Niche searches are won; town searches aren't.** Car club searches: #2 to #3 on Google, #3 on Bing. Hinsdale and Oak Brook searches: #14 to #17 on Google, behind an exact-match domain, Yelp, agencies with city pages and a map pack Elimenti isn't in.
- **ChatGPT already recommends Elimenti first** for "best web designers in Hinsdale", private car clubs and Hinsdale/Oak Brook med spas, but repeats copy removed on November 8 ("Projects start at $4,000", "hand-coded"), and never shows Elimenti in its map panel.
- **Gaps:** ChatGPT didn't mention Elimenti for WordPress maintenance in DuPage County or for Oak Brook; Gemini didn't mention it for Hinsdale.
- **Brave held one elimenti.com page, a stale one.** Claude's web search runs on Brave, so Claude could barely see the site.
- **Two outside sources contradict the site:** steponesites.com (the pre-rebrand site, still live, selling hand-coded sites at $175 a month) and hashim-hassan.com ("I hand-code static websites"). Both are outside this repo; see `seo/todo-hashim.md` items 3 and 4.

## Checks before merging

- `check.py` against a local server: all checks passed (188 URLs crawled, 47 indexable, 47 in the sitemap). It now normalizes FAQ text the same way `build.py` does.
- Schema Markup Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings on the homepage (the shared LocalBusiness block on every page), `/site-care/`, the new guide, `/guides/`, `/faq/`, `/hinsdale-web-design/`, `/menswear-store-websites/` and `/classic-car-restoration-websites/`.
- Overlap (5-word phrases, containment): highest pair on the site 14.0% (Hinsdale vs Oak Brook, 13.9% before). New guide vs disappeared-designer guide 1.1%, vs Site Care 2.3%. Site Care vs FAQ 6.8%.
- `llms.py --check`: up to date (47 pages). Titles ≤ 60 and descriptions ≤ 155 on every page.

## Decisions

1. **No "best web designers in Hinsdale" list ranking Elimenti first.** Lists are the most-cited page type in AI answers, but in Lily Ray's 2026 research, sites that ranked themselves first in their own "best" lists lost Google visibility (one with ten such pages dropped 29%; Google confirmed no update), and Google's AI answers cited those lists while leaving the publisher out of the recommendation 69% of the time.
2. **No Oak Brook page changes beyond one line.** For Oak Brook, ChatGPT named only firms with Oak Brook addresses and map reviews. That's a listings problem (`seo/todo-hashim.md` items 1 and 2), not a page problem.
3. **"Step One Sites" isn't an alternate name yet.** While steponesites.com still advertises $175-a-month hand-coded sites, tying the names together could pull that offer into Elimenti's answers. Add it after the redirect.
4. **Other repos untouched.** steponesites.com and hashim-hassan.com live in other repos this session wasn't authorized to change; the exact fixes are in `seo/todo-hashim.md`.
5. **Perplexity wasn't tested:** it asked for a sign-up.
