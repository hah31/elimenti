## Decisions

1. **No "best web designers in Hinsdale" list ranking Elimenti first.** Lists are the most-cited page type in AI answers, but in Lily Ray's 2026 research, sites that ranked themselves first in their own "best" lists lost Google visibility (one with ten such pages dropped 29%; Google confirmed no update), and Google's AI answers cited those lists while leaving the publisher out of the recommendation 69% of the time.
2. **No Oak Brook page changes beyond one line.** For Oak Brook, ChatGPT named only firms with Oak Brook addresses and map reviews. That's a listings problem (`seo/todo-hashim.md` items 1 and 2), not a page problem.
3. **"Step One Sites" isn't an alternate name yet.** While steponesites.com still advertises $175-a-month hand-coded sites, tying the names together could pull that offer into Elimenti's answers. Add it after the redirect. *(Done later the same day, PR #42, once steponesites.com redirected.)*
4. **Other repos untouched.** steponesites.com and hashim-hassan.com live in other repos this session wasn't authorized to change; the exact fixes are in `seo/todo-hashim.md`. *(Superseded: Hashim approved both later that day; see below.)*
5. **Perplexity wasn't tested:** it asked for a sign-up.
