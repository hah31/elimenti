The same was true on November 18 for car show, concours, collector car storage, classic car restoration shop, motorcycle club, yacht club, menswear store and calligraphy artist website design.

- Homepage "Redesigns" card links to `/website-redesign/`. Footer: Redesigns, Automotive, Menswear stores.
- Inbound links to every new page from related pages; guide index updated.
- `llms.py`: industry pages under a hub are matched by slug, since they aren't in the footer.

Sitemap: 38 → 46 URLs. `check.py`: all checks passed. Highest 5-word-phrase overlap for any new page: 12.5% (`/automotive-websites/` vs `/car-club-websites/`); goal is under 15%.
