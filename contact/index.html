Tell us about your business.

A few lines is enough: what you do, where you are and what the site needs to do. Every message is read by Hashim, who replies personally to set up a video call. If it's time-sensitive, call (630) 509-8822.

Or call (630) 509-8822 · hello@elimenti.com

What happens after you send it

1. A reply from Hashim
A personal reply, not an automated sequence, to find a time to talk.
2. A video call
About your business, your customers and what the site has to do. If Elimenti isn't the right fit, you'll hear that on the call.
3. A written proposal
Scope, price and timeline in writing, usually within a week of the call. No work starts until you've approved it.
4. The project
Copy, design and code, with up to three rounds of revisions. Most projects launch four to six weeks after kickoff. How a project runs →

What helps in your message

  • What your business does and who buys from you.
  • Your current website, if you have one, and what isn't working about it.
  • Any date the site needs to be live by, such as an opening or a season.
  • A rough budget. Projects start at $3,000, and most land between $3,500 and $4,500.

Already have a site that works and just needs someone to keep it current? Say so. Site Care covers existing WordPress sites too.
