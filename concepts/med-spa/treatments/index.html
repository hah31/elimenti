Treatments

Treatments and prices

Every treatment on the menu, with a starting price. Your exact cost depends on your plan, and you'll have it in writing before anything starts. New patients begin with a $50 consultation, credited to the first treatment.

Injectables

Performed by our nurse practitioner or RN injectors, under the medical director's protocols.

  • Neuromodulators

    From $14 per unit

    Botox, Dysport or Xeomin to soften forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet. Results last about three to four months.

  • Dermal filler

    From $700 per syringe

    Hyaluronic acid filler to restore volume in the cheeks, jawline and temples. Placed conservatively, built up over visits if needed.

  • Lip filler

    From $650

    Shape and hydration, in proportion with your face. Half-syringe appointments are available.

Skin

Treatments by our licensed esthetician, with a home routine to match.

  • Chemical peel

    From $150

    Light to medium-depth peels for dullness, uneven tone and early sun damage. Little to no downtime for light peels.

  • Microneedling

    From $350

    For texture, fine lines and acne scarring. Most people plan a series of three, a month apart.

  • Solenne facial

    From $175

    Seventy-five minutes: cleanse, exfoliation, extractions where needed and a mask chosen for your skin that day.

Laser

Performed by our nurse practitioner after a skin assessment and, for some treatments, a test spot.

  • Laser hair removal

    From $200 per area

    Underarms, legs, bikini line and face. Most areas need six or more sessions.

  • IPL photofacial

    From $400

    Fades redness, sun spots and broken capillaries. Best booked in fall and winter, away from strong sun.

  • Laser resurfacing

    From $600

    For deeper texture and lines. Plan for several days of redness and peeling.

Please give us 24 hours. Late cancellations are charged the consultation fee.
