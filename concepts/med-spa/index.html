Your first visit

No packages. A plan.

  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes with a provider. We look at your skin and your history, and ask what you want to change. $50, credited to your first treatment.

  2. A written plan

    Treatments, timing and cost, in writing, before anything happens. If something isn't right for you, we'll say so.

  3. Treatment

    The same day if it suits you, or booked when you're ready. Follow-up is included for injectables.

Results
