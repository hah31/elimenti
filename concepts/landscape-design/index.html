Gardens designed for the house, built for winter.

Featured project

A hillside terrace in Burr Ridge

Site
A wooded half-acre falling twelve feet from the house to the creek
Problem
No usable lawn, water running toward the foundation and a yard nobody used
Plan
Three terraces linked by wide stone steps, a fire terrace off the kitchen and a shade garden on the slope
Materials
Wisconsin fieldstone, bluestone treads, ferns, hellebores and native sedges

Four seasons

Planned for February, not just June

A garden here spends four months under frost. We design the bones first: evergreen structure, stone that looks good wet and paths that work in snow. The flowers come second.

Every plan includes a winter view from the windows you look out of most.
