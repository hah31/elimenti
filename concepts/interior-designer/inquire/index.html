Process

Four phases

You approve each phase before the next one starts. Nothing is ordered until you've signed off on the design.

  1. One

    Consultation

    We walk the house with you, measure the rooms in question and talk through how you use them. $350, credited to your design fee.

    90 minutes

  2. Two

    Design

    Floor plans, elevations, materials and every piece of furniture, presented together so you see the rooms whole. One round of revisions is included.

    6 to 10 weeks

  3. Three

    Procurement

    We place every order, track it, inspect it on arrival and store it until install. Custom upholstery sets the pace.

    10 to 16 weeks

  4. Four

    Installation

    Our team delivers, places, styles and hangs everything. We take the boxes with us.

    1 to 2 days

Investment

What it costs

Design is a flat fee, set in your proposal after the consultation, based on the number of rooms and the scope of work.

Furnishings are billed at our trade price plus a fixed percentage, itemized on every invoice.

Most full-service projects begin at $150,000 including furnishings. Single rooms begin at $40,000. If that's not where you are yet, say so in the form. We may be able to suggest a smaller first phase.
