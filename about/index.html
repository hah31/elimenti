About Elimenti

Elimenti is a web design studio based in Hinsdale, Illinois, run by me, Hashim H. I design websites for independent businesses, built on WordPress so you can edit them, or custom-coded. Copywriting and a basic SEO setup are included, and every project runs over video calls, so I take clients anywhere in the US.

Why Elimenti exists

The businesses I grew up around in the Hinsdale area sell on quality. Their stores, their food, their work. A lot of their websites run on the same templates as everyone else's, and customers can tell. Elimenti builds a different kind of website: designed around the business, written to sound like it and built so it stays current.

In November 2026 I tested the websites of 150 independent businesses from the Hinsdale and Oak Brook chambers of commerce on a phone, using Lighthouse, Google's open-source testing tool, and a set of checks of my own. The full report shows what I found, in aggregate, without naming anyone.

Who I am

I'm an engineering student at the University of Illinois, and I run Elimenti alongside my classes.

Before Elimenti, I built two other things:

  • Red Devils Garage. I co-founded this local car club and organized its charity car meet in the Hinsdale Central High School parking lot. More than 200 cars showed up, and we raised $10,000 for HCS Family Services.
  • Vitesse. An automotive media brand I built to more than 20 million organic views. Attention on a screen is won or lost in the first second, and that's the first job of a website too.

My first website work was for Burdi Clothing, the menswear boutique on Hinsdale Avenue, in 2025. I still make targeted edits to Burdi's existing site, including the event pages for its Father's Day and Toys for Tots car shows.

How the studio works

You work with me directly, from the first call through launch. There's no account manager in between and no work handed to a team you never meet. I write the proposal and the copy, design the site and build it, on WordPress or in custom code.

1. Call
A video call about your business, your customers and what the site has to do. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so on that call.
2. Proposal
Scope, price and timeline in writing before any work starts.
3. Design and copy
I write every page and design the full site. You get up to three rounds of revisions.
4. Launch
Built, tested on phones and desktops and launched on your domain with basic SEO in place. On WordPress, I walk you through editing the site yourself.
5. After launch
Site Care handles changes in five business days, on a monthly plan or by the hour. It's optional.

WordPress or custom code

Website builders like Squarespace and Wix put a template, hosting and an editor on one monthly plan, which suits an owner who wants to build the site themselves. I design each site from scratch and build it on WordPress or in custom code, and I'll recommend one on the first call:

  • WordPress, if you'll update it often. You or your staff edit text, photos, events and posts yourselves. There's a plugin for almost anything, any WordPress developer can take it over, and it needs regular updates, which Site Care covers. I already work in it: I maintain ABN Construction Group's WordPress site, built with Elementor, and add new sections to it.
  • Custom code, if it rarely changes. Very fast, with nothing to update and no dashboard to manage. Every change goes through Site Care. 1 of 1 Car Club is a full custom-coded build, and so is this site. Its homepage scores 100 in all four Lighthouse categories on mobile (performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO), measured with Lighthouse 13.6 on November 4, 2026. You can test it yourself at pagespeed.web.dev.

Either way, the layout, type and copy start from your business, and nothing is tied to my account. Neither is the right answer for everyone. Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or custom walks through when each one fits.

What Elimenti doesn't do

  • Online stores. I don't build them. I link your site to the store you already run.
  • Sites live by Friday. Copy, design and three revision rounds take weeks, not days.
  • Templates. Every site is designed and written from scratch and priced from its own scope. If a builder plan suits this year's budget better, the cost guide covers cheaper routes honestly.
  • Ads, social media or ongoing SEO campaigns. I build websites, with a basic SEO setup at launch. I don't run marketing.

What you keep

After the final payment, the site is yours. Your domain and hosting are in your name. On WordPress, you get admin access to the install, theme, content and plugins, with any premium licenses in your name, and any WordPress developer can take it over. On a custom-coded site, the code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can read. Either way, you're never locked in to me.
