About Elimenti

Elimenti is a web design studio based in Hinsdale and Oak Brook, Illinois, run by me, Hashim H. I design and custom-code websites for high-end independent businesses.

Why Elimenti exists

The businesses I grew up around in the Hinsdale area sell on quality. Their stores, their food, their work. A lot of their websites run on the same templates as everyone else's, and customers can tell. Elimenti builds a different kind of website: designed from scratch, custom-coded and written to sound like the business.

Where I started

My first website work was for Burdi Clothing, the menswear boutique on Hinsdale Avenue, in 2025. I still make targeted edits to Burdi's existing site, including the event pages for its Father's Day and Toys for Tots car shows.

I also co-founded Red Devils Garage, a local car club, and organized its charity car meet in the Hinsdale Central High School parking lot. More than 200 cars showed up, and we raised $10,000 for HCS Family Services.

How I work

I study engineering at the University of Illinois and run Elimenti alongside it. Every project runs remotely, which is what most clients prefer anyway.

You work with me directly, from the first call through launch. I write the proposal and the copy, design the site and build it. When you need a change later, I'm the one who answers the email, and Site Care requests are turned around in five business days.

What you keep

After the final payment, the site is yours. Your domain and hosting are in your name. The code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can read, so you're never locked in to me.

Tell me about your business.

Send a few lines about what you do and what the site needs to do. I'll reply to set up a call.
