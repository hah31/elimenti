About Elimenti

Elimenti is a web design studio based in Hinsdale and Oak Brook, Illinois, run by me, Hashim H. I design and custom-code websites for high-end independent businesses, with no templates or page builders. Copywriting and a basic SEO setup are included, projects start at $3,000, and every project runs over video calls, so I take clients anywhere in the US.

Why Elimenti exists

The businesses I grew up around in the Hinsdale area sell on quality. Their stores, their food, their work. A lot of their websites run on the same templates as everyone else's, and customers can tell. Elimenti builds a different kind of website: designed from scratch, custom-coded and written to sound like the business.

In November 2026 I checked the websites of independent businesses in the Hinsdale and Oak Brook chambers of commerce, one by one, with the same tools Google publishes for developers. The full report shows what I found, in aggregate, without naming anyone.

Who I am

I'm an engineering student at the University of Illinois, and I run Elimenti alongside my classes.

Before Elimenti, I built two things that taught me most of what I know about getting attention the right way:

  • Red Devils Garage. I co-founded this local car club and organized its charity car meet in the Hinsdale Central High School parking lot. More than 200 cars showed up, and we raised $10,000 for HCS Family Services.
  • Vitesse. An automotive media brand I built to more than 20 million organic views. It taught me how people actually look at a screen: what makes them stop, what makes them scroll past and how little time a first impression gets.

My first website work was for Burdi Clothing, the menswear boutique on Hinsdale Avenue, in 2025. I still make targeted edits to Burdi's existing site, including the event pages for its Father's Day and Toys for Tots car shows.

How the studio works

You work with me directly, from the first call through launch. There's no account manager in between and no work handed to a team you never meet. I write the proposal and the copy, design the site and build it.

1. Call
A video call about your business, your customers and what the site has to do. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so on that call.
2. Proposal
Scope, price and timeline in writing before any work starts.
3. Design and copy
I write every page and design the full site. You get up to three rounds of revisions.
4. Launch
Custom-coded, tested on phones and desktops and launched on your domain with basic SEO in place.
5. After launch
Site Care handles changes in five business days, at $175 a month or $85 an hour. It's optional.

Why custom code

A website builder rents you a template and a monthly plan. A custom-coded site is written for one business, and it does three things a template struggles to:

  • It loads fast on a phone. There's no builder code to haul around, only what the page needs. This site's homepage scores 100 in all four Lighthouse categories on mobile (performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO), measured with Lighthouse 13.6 on November 4, 2026. You can test it yourself at pagespeed.web.dev.
  • It looks like you. The layout, type and copy start from your business, not from a theme thousands of other businesses also use.
  • It belongs to you. The code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can read. Nothing is tied to my account or to a platform's subscription.

Custom code isn't always the right answer. Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or custom walks through when each one fits.

Who Elimenti isn't for

  • You want to edit the site yourself every week. A builder like Squarespace suits that better. Updates here go through Site Care.
  • You need a full online store. I don't build stores. I can link your site to one you already run.
  • You need a site live by Friday. Copy, design and three revision rounds take weeks, not days.
  • You're after the lowest price. Projects start at $3,000. If that's more than the site needs to earn back, the cost guide covers cheaper routes honestly.
  • You want ads, social media or an ongoing SEO campaign. I build websites, with a basic SEO setup at launch. I don't run marketing.

What you keep

After the final payment, the site is yours. Your domain and hosting are in your name. The code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can read, so you're never locked in to me. What to do if a web designer disappears explains why that matters.
