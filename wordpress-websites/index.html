Page builder or no page builder

We decide per project, and the proposal says which. A page builder like Elementor lets you change a page's layout yourself, not just its words. Building without one means fewer moving parts to keep updated, and you edit the content inside a layout we've set. Which one fits depends on how much of the site you'll want to rearrange yourself.

We work both ways. We maintain ABN Construction Group's WordPress site, which is built with Elementor, and add new sections to it inside the existing design. And we build without page builders entirely: 1 of 1 Car Club is a full custom-coded build.

Editing the site yourself

You log in and change what changes most: text, photos, events and posts. At launch, we walk you through how, on your own site, so the first edit isn't a guess.

Anything you'd rather not do yourself goes through Site Care: a new page, a new section, a layout change or a plugin you want added. Plenty of owners do a bit of both.

Site Care: updates and backups

WordPress, its theme and its plugins all release updates, and a site that skips them gets harder to keep safe and working. On the $175 monthly plan, Site Care covers WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, plus backups, alongside up to four content updates a month, each an hour or less. One-off edits are $85 an hour. Turnaround is five business days.

Site Care is optional. If you leave it, the site keeps running, and you or another developer take on the updates.

Ownership, licenses and hosting

  • Admin access. You get admin access to your own WordPress install, theme, content and plugins. Nothing is held on our accounts.
  • Licenses in your name. If the site needs a premium plugin or theme, the license is in your name and you pay for it directly, the same way you pay for hosting.
  • Hosting in your name. We recommend WordPress-capable hosting and set it up, on your account.
  • No lock-in. WordPress is open-source and widely used. Any WordPress developer can take the site over.

WordPress or custom-coded?

Both start at $4,000, and neither is the lesser option. WordPress suits owners or volunteers who'll update the site often: you edit it yourself, there's a plugin for almost anything, and it needs regular updates. A custom-coded site suits a business whose site rarely changes and needs to feel as sharp as possible: it's very fast, with nothing to update and no dashboard, and every change goes through Site Care. Questions about WordPress sites

Can I really edit the site myself?

Yes. You get your own login and a walkthrough at launch of how to edit text, photos, events and posts. Bigger changes, like a new page layout, can go through Site Care instead.

Do you build with Elementor?

When it suits the project. We decide per project, and the proposal says which way we'll build. We already maintain ABN Construction Group's WordPress site, built with Elementor, and add new sections to it.

Who pays for hosting and plugins?

You do, directly. Hosting, your domain and any premium plugin or theme licenses are on accounts in your name. We recommend the hosting and set it up for you.

Do I need Site Care?

No, but a WordPress site does need its updates. The $175 monthly plan covers WordPress core, theme and plugin updates and backups, plus up to four content updates a month. Without it, you or another developer keep the site updated.

Can you build an online store on WordPress?

No. Elimenti doesn't build online stores. If you already sell through a store platform, we link your new site to it.
