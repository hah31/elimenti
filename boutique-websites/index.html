Websites for independent boutiques and showrooms

An independent boutique's website should feel like the store: photo-led, quiet and fast on a phone, with hours, location and what you carry on the first screen. Elimenti designs sites for boutiques, showrooms and specialty shops in Hinsdale and the Chicago suburbs, on WordPress or custom-coded, linked to your online store if you have one. Projects start at $4,000.

What a boutique site has to do

  • Answer the first questions fast: where you are, when you're open, what you carry.
  • Let the photos sell. The product and the space should carry the page, not stock images or filler text.
  • Make it easy to book an appointment or come to an event.
  • Point to your online store if you sell online. We don't build stores, but we link your site to the one you run.

Where templates fall short for boutiques

  • Store templates for a store that isn't online. Platforms push product grids, carts and checkout. If your sale happens in the fitting room, those features get in the way of the site's real job: getting people through the door.
  • Everyone's layout. The same header, grid and fonts as thousands of other shops. A boutique sells on not looking like everyone else.
  • Events buried in a blog. Trunk shows, launches and in-store events end up three clicks deep, or still on the homepage after they've happened.
  • Hours that drift. Holiday hours on the site and on Google stop matching, and customers find out at a locked door.

If you sell online too

Keep your store where it is. If you sell through Shopify or another store platform, we design the website around your shop, your brand and your events, and link to your store for online orders. Customers get one brand with two doors.

WordPress or custom-coded for a boutique

Stock themes look alike, and a boutique sells on not looking like everyone else. Either way, the site is designed around your brand, your photos and your customers. The difference is who changes it.

On WordPress, you post events, new arrivals and seasonal photos yourself. A custom-coded site is very fast, with nothing to update and no dashboard to manage, and every change goes through Site Care, turned around in five business days. If the shop changes its site every week, choose WordPress. If the site rarely changes and needs to feel as sharp as the store, choose custom-coded.

How local shoppers find you matters as much as the site itself: local SEO for a boutique in the suburbs covers your Business Profile, reviews and the pages worth having.

Questions boutique owners ask

Can you build my online store?

No. Elimenti doesn't build online stores or checkout systems. If you sell through Shopify or another platform, we link your new website to that store so customers can shop there, and we design the site so the two feel like one brand.

How do new arrivals and events get on the site?

On a WordPress site, you post them yourself with your own login. On a custom-coded site, or whenever you'd rather hand it off, you send the photos and details through Site Care, and the update is live within five business days, at $175 a month or $85 an hour. For things that change daily, like new arrivals, we link to the channel you already update, such as your store or Instagram.

Can you photograph the shop?

Yes, as an optional add-on at $85 an hour. Photos of the space, the product and the people do more for a boutique website than anything else. If you already have strong photography, we design around it.
