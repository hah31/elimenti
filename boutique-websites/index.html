Websites for independent boutiques and showrooms

Elimenti designs custom-coded websites for independent boutiques, showrooms and specialty shops. Each site is built to feel like the store itself: photo-led, quiet and fast on a phone.

What a boutique site has to do

  • Answer the first questions fast: where you are, when you're open, what you carry.
  • Let the photos sell. The product and the space should carry the page, not stock images or filler text.
  • Make it easy to book an appointment or come to an event.
  • Point to your online store if you sell online. We don't build stores, but we link your site to the one you run.

Our boutique work

We handle Site Care for the WordPress site of Burdi Clothing, a luxury Italian menswear boutique in Hinsdale with a 50-year history. That includes the event pages for its Father's Day Functional Art Car Show and its December Toys for Tots car show. We didn't build Burdi's site. We keep it current, and events go up on time. Why custom-coded for a boutique

Theme-built sites look alike, and a boutique sells on not looking like everyone else. A custom-coded site is designed around your brand, your photos and your customers, and it loads fast because it isn't carrying a builder's extra weight. The tradeoff: you don't edit it yourself. Changes go through Site Care, turned around in five business days.

Pricing and next step

Everything is scoped to the project, and projects start at $3,000. Copywriting and a basic SEO setup are included, and photography is available at $85 an hour.
