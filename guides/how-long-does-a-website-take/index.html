How long a custom website takes

How long a custom website takes depends mostly on its size and on how fast the content comes together. Most Elimenti projects launch four to six weeks after kickoff. One Chicago agency publishes one to two months for its starter build and three to six for its fully custom one. Content, not code, is what adds weeks.

By Hashim H., Elimenti. Published November 4, 2026. Updated November 8, 2026.

The stages, and what each one waits on

Every stage is quick when it has what it needs. Projects slow down at the hand-offs, when one side is waiting on the other.

1. First call
Your business, your customers and what the site has to do. Waits on: finding a time.
2. Proposal
Scope, price and timeline in writing, usually within a week of the call. Waits on: your approval.
3. Copy and design
Every page written and the full site designed, then up to three rounds of revisions. Waits on: your answers about the business, your photos and your feedback on each round.
4. Build and testing
The approved design built, on WordPress or in custom code, and tested on phones and desktops. Waits on: very little. This is the part that runs on the studio's schedule.
5. Launch
The site goes live on your domain with basic SEO in place. Waits on: access to your domain registrar.

Changes after launch go through Site Care, with a five-business-day turnaround.

What's built into our process

  • We write the copy. Copywriting is included, so you aren't stuck drafting pages between other work. You answer questions; we write.
  • One person, start to finish. There's no hand-off between a salesperson, a designer and a developer.
  • Defined revision rounds. Up to three, each with a clear set of changes, instead of open-ended back and forth.
  • A timeline in writing. The proposal states it before any work starts.

Before kickoff: a checklist

  • Your logo files, in the best quality you have
  • Photos you own, or a plan for photography
  • Your services and prices, even as a rough list
  • Two or three websites you like, and what you like about them
  • The name of the one person who signs off
  • Your domain registrar login
  • Any date the site must be live by, such as an opening or a season

Common questions

Can a custom website be built in a week?

Not with the copy written for you and three rounds of revisions. A template site can go up faster, because the design and structure already exist. A custom one is designed around your business, and that takes weeks, not days.

Does a redesign take less time than a new site?

Sometimes. Existing photos and copy can save time, but a redesign also means planning which old pages move, merge or redirect so you don't lose visitors who arrive at old addresses.

What if I'm slow to send things?

The launch date moves with it. Content is the biggest variable in any website project, which is why the checklist above is worth doing before kickoff.
