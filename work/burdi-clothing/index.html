Burdi Clothing: Site Care

Burdi Clothing is a luxury Italian menswear boutique on Hinsdale Avenue in Hinsdale, Illinois. Elimenti provides Site Care for its existing WordPress website, billed by the hour.

At a glance

Client
Burdi Clothing, Hinsdale
Scope
Site Care for an existing WordPress site. We didn't build the site, and the online store is outside our scope.
Since
2025
Status
Ongoing, as needed

The brief

Burdi runs events as well as a store, from car shows to book signings, and its website's events page is where customers check the details. New events need to go up on time, and nothing else on the site can break when they do.

What we did

  • Father's Day Functional Art Car Show 2026. Wrote and published the event page for Burdi's annual Father's Day car show, which benefited the Hinsdale Humane Society.
  • Toys for Tots car show. Built and published the listing for Burdi's December Toys for Tots car show.
  • Kept the rest intact. Every edit goes into the existing WordPress site without changing its design or touching the store.
