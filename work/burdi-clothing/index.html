Burdi Clothing: targeted website edits

Burdi Clothing, legally Burdi European Boutique Inc., is a luxury Italian menswear boutique on Hinsdale Avenue in Hinsdale, Illinois. Elimenti makes targeted edits to its existing WordPress site, billed by the hour, most visibly the pages for the store's charity car shows.

The store and its customers

Burdi describes itself as 50 years of luxury Italian menswear, sold from its store at 24 W. Hinsdale Avenue. The website backs the store up rather than replacing it. Its menu is short: About, Location, Events and Book an Appointment.

What's coming up is often a car show. Burdi's Father's Day Functional Art Car Show reached its third year in 2026, with proceeds going to the Hinsdale Humane Society. Its December Toys for Tots drive, run with the Marine Toys for Tots Foundation, reached its sixth year in 2025. The events page is where people check the details.

The problem

An event page has a deadline the rest of a website doesn't. It has to be live before people start asking about the date, written in the store's voice, and right on the details: the day, the time, the address, who benefits and how to register. And on a working WordPress site, an edit can't risk breaking the pages that sell clothes.

Burdi didn't need a new website. It needed someone to make these changes well, on time, without touching anything else.

What we did, and why

  • Father's Day Functional Art Car Show 2026. Wrote and published the event page. The registration and donation link is the first line, above the story, because it's the action the page exists for. The date, hours, address and beneficiary sit in plain text, not just inside a flyer image, so search engines, screen readers and phones can all read them.
  • Toys for Tots car show. Wrote and published the page for Burdi's December Toys for Tots car show, with the schedule for exhibitors, spectators and the arriving caravan spelled out.
  • Wrote in Burdi's voice. Short, confident lines about the cars and the cause.
  • Kept the rest intact. Each edit goes into the existing theme without changing its design or touching the store.

The Toys for Tots 2025 page, captured November 4, 2026.

What you can check

Both pages are live: the Father's Day Functional Art Car Show 2026 and Toys for Tots 2025. Everything stated about the events above comes from those pages.

The takeaway

A site that works doesn't need a rebuild just because it needs changes. Targeted edits, billed by the hour, keep a working site current for a fraction of the cost of starting over.
