Pricing and process

Elimenti websites start at $4,000, whether they're built on WordPress or custom-coded. That includes design, copywriting, a basic SEO setup and up to three rounds of revisions. Every project is scoped individually, you get the exact price in a written proposal before work starts, and the site is yours after the final payment.

What moves the price

  • How many kinds of page you need. Ten treatment pages built from one design cost far less than ten different layouts.
  • How much there is to write. Copy is included, and a long service menu takes longer to write well.
  • Photography. Whether we design around photos you have or shoot new ones.
  • Connections. Linking to your booking platform or online store is simple. Memberships, event registration and anything more custom are scoped in the proposal.
  • Moving old content. Carrying over a large archive of pages, posts or files from an old site is scoped and priced in the proposal.
  • Visual identity. A new logo and type system alongside the site adds to the price and the timeline.

How a project runs

1. Call
A video call about your business, your customers and what the site has to do. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so here.
2. Proposal
Scope, price and timeline in writing, before any work starts.
3. Design and copy
We write every page and design the full site, then you get up to three rounds of revisions.
4. Launch
Built on WordPress or custom-coded, tested on phones and desktops, and launched on your domain with basic SEO in place. On WordPress, you get your login and a walkthrough of how to edit the site.
5. Site Care
Changes after launch, turned around in five business days.

Most projects launch four to six weeks after kickoff. The biggest variable is content: a site moves as fast as photos, details and feedback come back.

Payment and ownership

You pay by card through Stripe or by check. After the final payment, the site is yours, and the domain and hosting are in your name. You're never locked in to us.

  • On WordPress, you get admin access to your own WordPress install, theme, content and plugins. Premium plugin or theme licenses are in your name, and you pay for them directly, the same way you pay for hosting. Any WordPress developer can take the site over.
  • On a custom-coded site, the code is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can work on.

WordPress needs hosting that can run it. We recommend a host and set it up for you, on an account in your name.
