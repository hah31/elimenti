Custom website design, on WordPress or custom-coded

A custom website is designed around one business, then built one of two ways: on WordPress, so you can edit it yourself, or custom-coded in HTML, CSS and JavaScript for maximum speed. Elimenti designs them for high-end businesses in Chicago, its western suburbs and anywhere in the US. Both start at $4,000 and include copywriting, a basic SEO setup and up to three rounds of revisions.

Two ways to build it

Neither is the cheap option or the premium one. Both start at $4,000. The difference is who changes the site after launch.

WordPress
You can edit text, photos, events and posts yourself. A huge range of plugins covers things like memberships, calendars and forms, and any WordPress developer can take the site over. It needs regular updates to WordPress, the theme and the plugins, which Site Care covers. About WordPress websites →
Custom-coded
Written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript for one business. Very fast, with nothing to update and no dashboard to manage. Every change goes through Site Care, turned around in five business days.

You, your staff or your volunteers will update the site often.
Choose custom-coded if
The site rarely changes and needs to feel as sharp as possible.

Why design it around your business

A stock design is built to work for anyone, which means it was never built for you. Customers who pay for quality notice when a site looks like a thousand others, and they notice when it loads slowly on a phone.

A custom design starts from how your customers decide. A med spa needs a page for every treatment. A builder needs projects sorted by type. A boutique needs its photos to carry the page. A club needs its members, events and archive in one place. That holds on WordPress and in custom code alike.

Who we build for

Independent businesses whose customers judge them on quality: the product, the service, the room they walk into. We've written up what the site needs to do for each of these:

  • Med spas: a page per treatment, booking one tap away, credentials stated plainly.
  • Interior designers: project pages with a story, and an inquiry form that qualifies.
  • Custom home builders: galleries split by type of work, a process page and a clear service area.
  • Landscape design firms: projects shown by the problem they solved, design-build kept apart from maintenance.
  • Boutiques and showrooms: photo-led, with hours, events and a link to your store.
  • Private car clubs: a collection that grows and an application that asks the right questions.
  • Classic and marque car clubs: members-only areas, renewals, chapters, events and a technical library.

We're not the right fit if you need a full online store. We link your site to the store you already run.

Redesigns

If you have a Wix or Squarespace site that looks like its template, a Joomla site or an old static HTML site nobody wants to touch, or a WordPress theme that has slowed down and breaks when it's edited, we redesign it. WordPress is the default for a redesign, so the new site is yours to edit. Your domain comes with you, and we carry over the content worth keeping and rewrite the rest.
