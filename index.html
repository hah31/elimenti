What we do

WordPress websites

Designed around your business and built on WordPress, so you can edit text, photos, events and posts yourself. You get your own login and a walkthrough at launch. Copy, basic SEO and up to three rounds of revisions are included. About WordPress websites →

Custom-coded websites

Written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript for one business. Very fast, with nothing to update and no dashboard to manage. Changes go through Site Care. Same inclusions, scoped the same way. WordPress or custom-coded? →

Redesigns

For businesses moving off Wix, Squarespace, Joomla, an aging WordPress theme or an old static HTML site. WordPress is the default for a redesign, so the new site is yours to edit. Your domain and your content come with you. Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or custom? →

Site Care

Changes after launch, with a five-business-day turnaround. The monthly plan is the one we recommend, and on WordPress sites it also covers core, theme and plugin updates and backups. Individual edits are billed by the hour. We also care for existing WordPress sites. About Site Care →

Visual identity and photography

Your logo, typefaces and color system, built so the website, the storefront and everything you print look like one business. Photos of your space, your product and your work, shot for the site. Both are scoped in the same proposal as the website.

Who we build for

Boutiques, builders, med spas, restaurants, car clubs, artists and professional firms. If your customers judge you on the quality of what you make, they'll judge your website the same way.

Selling online? We link your site to the store you already run instead of building one.

How a project runs

  1. Call.

    A conversation about your business, your customers and what the site has to do.

  2. Proposal.

    Scope, price and timeline in writing, before any work starts.

  3. Design and copy.

    We write every page and design the full site. You get up to three rounds of revisions.

  4. Launch.

    Built, tested on phones and desktops, and launched on your domain with basic SEO in place. WordPress sites come with your login and a walkthrough.

  5. Site Care.

    Updates after launch, turned around in five business days.

Every project is scoped individually. You get the exact price and timeline in a written proposal before any work starts.
