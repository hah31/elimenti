What we do

Custom websites

Designed from scratch and custom-coded in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. We write the copy, set up the basic SEO and give you up to three rounds of revisions. After the final payment, the site is yours.

Redesigns

For businesses moving off Wix, Squarespace or an aging WordPress theme. We rebuild the site from scratch in custom code. Your domain and your content come with you.

Visual identity

Your logo, typefaces and color system, built so the website, the storefront and everything you print look like one business. Quoted per project.

Site Care

Changes after launch, with a five-business-day turnaround. The $175 monthly plan is the one we recommend. Individual edits are $85 an hour. We also care for existing WordPress sites.

Photography (add-on)

Photos of your space, your product and your work, shot for the site. $85 an hour.

Built for businesses that compete on quality.

Boutiques, builders, restaurants, car clubs, artists and professional firms. If your customers judge you by the quality of what you make, they judge your website the same way. A theme that a thousand other businesses use won't hold up to that.

We're not the right fit if you want to edit the site yourself every week or need a full online store. We'll tell you that on the first call.

How a project runs

  1. Call.

    A conversation about your business, your customers and what the site has to do.

  2. Proposal.

    Scope, price and timeline in writing, before any work starts.

  3. Design and copy.

    We write every page and design the full site. You get up to three rounds of revisions.

  4. Launch.

    Custom-coded, tested on phones and desktops, and launched on your domain with basic SEO in place.

  5. Site Care.

    Updates after launch, turned around in five business days.

Everything is scoped to the project.
